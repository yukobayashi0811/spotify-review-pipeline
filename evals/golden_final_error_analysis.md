# Golden 50: error analysis of the final labels

Generated by `scripts/golden_error_analysis.py` (no model calls) from `evals/golden_50.csv` (human labels) and `evals/golden_final_predictions.csv` (the final full-run labels for those 50 reviews). Per-case detail: `evals/golden_final_error_cases.csv`. Headline agreement is in `evals/golden_final_evaluation.json` (topic 0.82, intent 0.80, exact severity 0.64, severity within ±1 0.92).

Failure categories below were assigned by reading each disagreement (analyst: the AI assistant that built the pipeline). The human labels are unchanged; where a disagreement looks like a boundary case in the shared definitions, that is stated rather than counted as a correction.

## Where the errors are

- Rows with at least one wrong field (topic, intent or severity): 25 of 50.
- By final label source: local classifier 24 of 48; GPT-6 Luna 1 of 2.
- Not in English (identified by reading): 4 rows (Indonesian, Tagalog, romanized Hindi/Urdu), 4 with an error.
- `needs_review` was set on 16 rows; 12 of the 25 error rows had it (see `evals/golden_needs_review_analysis.json`).

| Category | Rows | What happens |
|---|---:|---|
| blocked_core_task_scored_3_not_4 | 5 | Playback or song choice is blocked ('cannot play', 'plays a little then stops'); the human used 4 (core task blocked), the model 3 (degraded). The local classifier rarely predicts 4. |
| specific_feature_vs_generic_other | 4 | Short praise or criticism that names a feature in passing (ads, diversity, updates, collections): the human picked the specific topic, the model 'other' or a neighbouring topic. |
| generic_criticism_severity_1_vs_2 | 4 | Content-free criticism ('worst app', 'I hate this app'). The human used 1; the model used 2, which is what the shared definition gives for generic criticism, so these are boundary cases rather than clear model errors. |
| non_english_or_code_mixed | 4 | Indonesian, Tagalog and romanized Hindi/Urdu reviews. The TF-IDF classifier has little vocabulary for them; every such Golden case has at least one wrong field. |
| paid_plan_or_price_mention_pulled_to_billing | 3 | A Premium or price word moves the topic to billing even when the complaint is about ads or usability ('Duo Premium ... No ads at all!' is praise for usability). The contract says a paid-plan mention alone is not billing. |
| boycott_or_political_intent | 2 | Boycott or political statements: whether they express a personal departure (cancellation) or are a bare slogan (unclear) is ambiguous; the model chose unclear. |
| degraded_function_scored_2_not_3 | 1 | Restricted functionality ('limited functionality ... too much') scored as an annoyance (2) instead of degraded function (3). |
| edited_review_resolved_update | 1 | An edited review whose 'Update:' says Support fixed the problem, followed by the 'Old Post' describing a blocking playback failure. The human labeled the original problem (playback complaint, severity 4); GPT-6 Luna labeled the update (support praise, severity 1). The label definitions do not say which part of an edited review governs, so this is a definition gap rather than a clear model error. |
| unclear_vs_praise | 1 | Content too thin to decide between praise and unclear. |

## Evidence quotes and entities

- Quote is an exact substring of the review: 50/50 (checked in code).
- Quote is the whole review text: 43/50. The local classifier copies the text (or its first sentence) rather than selecting the clause that supports the label, so for long reviews a valid quote can be a weak justification; the quote is mechanically valid in every case, and whether it supports the label is part of the per-case review above.
- Quote supports the predicted label (read for all 50): yes 41, partially 5, no 4. In every 'no' row the quote is fine and the label is wrong (the quote is the review's own text); details in the `quote_supports_label` / `quote_support_note` columns of the cases file.
- Predicted entities not found in the text (unsupported additions): 0 rows.
- Entity list is only the brand name 'Spotify': 9 rows; the human lists name product features instead, which is the main reason entity exact match is 0.48.

## What changed because of this analysis

- The billing boundary (`premium-only controls are billing; a paid-plan mention alone is not`) was added to the verifier prompt and `config/labels.md` so the verifier applies the same rule as the classifier.
- The risk router sends rows the local classifier scores as severity ≥ 4, support, or billing/privacy terms to GPT-6 Luna; blocked-playback reviews that the local model scores 3 are not caught by that rule, which is a known limitation.
- Non-English reviews are a known weakness of the local classifier and are reported as a limitation rather than filtered out.
