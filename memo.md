## Limitations

- The evidence is a historical, self-selected app-review sample and may not represent current Spotify users or their concerns.
- The complete customer population, revenue, plan tier, confirmed churn, and causal retention data are absent.
- Cancellation language in reviews expresses intent; it is not evidence of observed churn.
- The supplied independent-verifier figures are estimates from 300 distinct texts: quote-supported rate 0.9766666666666667, row-weighted intent agreement 0.8382387347164736, exact severity agreement 0.7414117644971822, and topic agreement 0.7247775093933975. They are not human ground truth; the Luna figures are same-model test-retest, not independent accuracy.
- The sensitivity estimates apply a verifier-sample correction model. The baseline top issue I_USABILITY_04 has adjusted top probability 0.466, while I_OTHER_01 has 0.534; the adjusted topic remains usability with probability 1.0. Supplied 95% intervals reflect verifier-sample uncertainty under this model, not total model uncertainty, and the model cannot recover false-negative complaints into a specific issue.
- Grouping remains imperfect: embedding_v2 was adopted for a higher overall membership-weighted coherent rate (0.671791601391912 versus 0.46149020532087454), yet only 19 of 41 issues are marked coherent and some top-ranked issues are incoherent. The selected issue is marked coherent, but broader grouping incoherence remains. Baseline issue metrics use complaint and cancellation records only.
